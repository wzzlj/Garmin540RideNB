## Candidate English description

Ride540NB combines large speed and power numbers with ride and lap statistics on one full-screen Edge 540 data page. A configurable sprint screen focuses on speed, instantaneous power, cadence, heart rate and ride time. Device metric/statute distance units and elevation units are followed automatically. Night colors follow device settings; the clock can use 12- or 24-hour time.
